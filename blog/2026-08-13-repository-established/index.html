This repository now holds the teaching material that was previously spread across a personal site, a bookdown project, and a course repository from last spring. It is meant to be the one place either of us looks for a chapter, a deck, or a dataset.

What is here

Two readers of mine — Linear Regression Analysis (POL 682) and Advanced Regression and Causal Inference (POL 683) — plus fourteen reveal.js decks, the midterm and final with answer keys, and the datasets those materials depend on. The joint reader exists as a three-chapter spine.

The one structural decision

Each author owns a directory outright. Nothing in readers/weber/ is edited by anyone but me, and nothing in readers/westerland/ is edited by anyone but Chad. The joint reader does not copy from either — it cross-references them.

This is the whole reason the layout looks the way it does. Two people writing a methods book will otherwise spend a surprising fraction of their time resolving merge conflicts in prose, which is a bad use of anyone’s semester.

What changed in the port

POL 683 moved from bookdown to Quarto, so both readers now share one toolchain and one theme. Five bookdown template leftovers and two duplicate chapter drafts were dropped rather than migrated. Data loads that pointed at absolute paths on my laptop now resolve through here::here("shared/data/..."), which means they work on someone else’s machine — a precondition for this being a shared repository at all.

Three count-data examples still reference research data that is not in this repo and was not copied here. Those chunks do not run. See docs/PORTING-NOTES.md.
